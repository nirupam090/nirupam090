- 🏆 **3× National 1st Prize Winner**: Shark Tank 2026 (Fr. CRCE Mumbai), VentureFest 2026 (SRM Chennai), and Incubex 2026 Grand Prix (MIT-WPU Pune).

- 📝 **Published Author**: Lead author of *"ChatGPT: From Natural Language Processing to Responsible AI"* published in IJSREM (January 2024).
